Privacy Policy

WA Client Hub stores only the data needed to operate workspace access, billing status, and WhatsApp browser profile management.

Data we store

  • User names and emails
  • Password hashes
  • Workspace/member/role details
  • WhatsApp number labels and phone numbers
  • Billing provider/status metadata
  • Audit logs

WhatsApp content

The app does not intentionally collect, scrape, or store WhatsApp chat content. Browser profiles may contain active WhatsApp Web sessions and must be treated as sensitive.

Payments

Payment card details should be handled by the selected payment provider. WA Client Hub should not store card numbers or payment secrets.

Security

Use HTTPS, secure cookies, strong secrets, and protected remote desktop access in production.

Retention

Audit logs and account records may be retained for support, security, and billing operations.

Contact

Contact the workspace administrator for privacy requests.
