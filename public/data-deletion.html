Data Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your WA Client Hub account and associated personal data by contacting support@10xdigitalventures.com.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@10xdigitalventures.com with the subject WA Client Hub data deletion request.
  2. Include the email address used for your account, your workspace name, and the data you want removed.
  3. Send the request from the account email when possible. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership before deleting data. Never send a password, access token, or secret by email.

Processing timeline

We aim to acknowledge requests within 3 business days and complete verified deletion requests within 30 calendar days. If a request is unusually complex or a longer period is required by applicable law, we will explain the reason and expected timing.

What we remove

  • Your WA Client Hub account profile and workspace memberships
  • Workspace and WhatsApp number metadata associated with your account, such as labels and phone numbers
  • Connected-session references and stored provider credentials, subject to secure revocation and retention requirements
  • Other personal data that is not required for security, fraud prevention, billing, legal compliance, or dispute resolution

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, billing, legal obligations, unresolved disputes, or to document that a deletion request was completed. Backups and operational logs may take additional time to expire under their retention cycle.

Third-party services

Data held directly by WhatsApp, Meta, payment providers, remote-desktop providers, or other connected services may require a separate deletion request to that provider. We will identify relevant third-party data where practical.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions, contact support@10xdigitalventures.com.
