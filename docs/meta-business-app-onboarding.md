Verified on 22 September 2026 for Meta app 3543212862483951.

## Completed configuration and implementation

- Existing test connection token verified and rotated in encrypted storage. Meta identifies the new credential as SYSTEM_USER, with expiry 21 November 2026. No credential belongs in this document or Git.
- Live app webhook fields: messages, calls, history, smb_app_state_sync, smb_message_echoes.
- Embedded Signup offers Business App coexistence or an ordinary Cloud API number when META_COEXISTENCE_ENABLED=true.
- Coexistence launcher sends featureType=whatsapp_business_app_onboarding and sessionInfoVersion=3. Use the Meta v4 login configuration selected in the developer console; session info version is a different setting.
- FINISH_WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_APP_ONBOARDING may return only a WABA ID. The backend exchanges the code and resolves exactly one verified Business App/CLOUD_API number. Ambiguous or paginated selection fails closed.
- Installation persists jobs for WABA subscription, contact sync and history sync. These execute in order. The existing Business App number is never registered or deregistered by this flow.
- One-shot sync requests retain request IDs. Interrupted/time-out requests become uncertain, and are not blindly repeated. Pending jobs expire after 24 hours; request acceptance does not mean all history has arrived.
- Signed webhooks ingest app-sent echoes, contacts and history. Imported messages do not increment unread counts, create incoming outbox events, invoke inbound callbacks or start GHL workflows. Media-placeholder metadata can be reconciled by subsequent history payloads.
- Removing a mobile contact marks its source record removed; it does not delete CRM conversations. History-sharing refusal is recorded and respected.

## Connect the real phone after review/setup

Open Workspaces, choose the intended workspace, select **Keep using my WhatsApp Business app**, then **Connect WhatsApp with Meta**. In Meta, choose the existing Business App account/number. Follow the official in-app verification prompt (code or QR as Meta displays), choose whether to share history, and complete consent. Keep the mobile app open while sync runs. Check the connection, sync templates, then enable messaging and verify one authorized send and reply.

Do not delete the mobile WhatsApp account or use a generic registration/migration flow to force coexistence.
