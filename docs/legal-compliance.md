# Legal and compliance notes

This project is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

## Added pages

- `/terms.html`
- `/privacy.html`

## Key policies

- Manual use only.
- No spam, scraping, fraud, or unauthorized automation.
- No storage of payment card data in WA Client Hub.
- Browser profiles are sensitive because they may contain WhatsApp sessions.
- Remote desktop must be secured; raw RDP must not be public.

## Production note

Before selling publicly, have local legal counsel review these pages and update company name, contact details, jurisdiction, refund terms, and data retention period.
